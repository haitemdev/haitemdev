### 🚀 About Me

I am a **Software & Systems Engineer** with a multi-disciplinary academic and practical foundation across **Computer Science**, **Automatic Control & Robotics**, and **Artificial Intelligence**.

* 🔭 **Core Focus:** Architecting robust backend systems, RESTful microservices, computational energy models, and autonomous AI agents.
* ⚡ **Flagship Work:** Author of [**SolarForge**](https://github.com/haitemdev/solarforge), a dual-engine photovoltaic modeling backend built with **Ruby on Rails 8** and **Python (FastAPI / NumPy)**.
* 🧠 **Specializations:** Mathematical and dynamic system simulation, control theory (MATLAB/Simulink), LLM agent workflows, local model fine-tuning APIs, and Linux server administration.
* 🎓 **Education:** 
  * *Politechnika Białostocka* — B.Sc. Engineering (*Inżynier*) in Automatic Control & Robotics
  * *University of Colorado Boulder* — Pathway to Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence
  * *Université de Picardie Jules Verne (UPJV)* — Licence 3 in Electrical Energy & Automation (EEA)
  * *USTHB* — Bachelor in Computer Science
* 🌍 **Location:** Poland (Warsaw / Gliwice) — Open to B2B & Hybrid Collaborations.
